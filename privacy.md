# **privacy.md**  
## **FreeMailBox Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated:** September 2026

FreeMailBox is a local desktop email client that connects directly to Gmail using IMAP. FreeMailBox does not operate a cloud service, does not store user data on external servers, and does not share information with third parties.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FreeMailBox accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

---

## **1. Information We Access**

FreeMailBox connects to your Gmail account using Google OAuth. When you grant permission, FreeMailBox receives:

- Your Gmail email address  
- An OAuth access token  
- An OAuth refresh token  
- Permission to access Gmail via IMAP (`https://mail.google.com/`)

FreeMailBox does **not** access:

- Your Google Drive  
- Your Google Contacts  
- Your Google Calendar  
- Your Google Photos  
- Any other Google services

---

## **2. How Your Data Is Used**

FreeMailBox uses the Gmail IMAP API to:

- Read your email messages  
- Download message metadata  
- Download message bodies  
- Download attachments (only when you open them)

FreeMailBox does **not**:

- Modify your Gmail account  
- Delete messages  
- Send email  
- Access any data outside Gmail

---

## **3. Local‑Only Storage**

All data retrieved from Gmail is stored **locally on your device**. This includes:

- Email messages  
- Attachments  
- OAuth tokens  
- Account configuration

FreeMailBox does **not**:

- Upload data to any server  
- Sync data to the cloud  
- Transmit data to the developer  
- Use analytics, telemetry, or tracking

You have full control over your data at all times.

---

## **4. No Third‑Party Sharing**

FreeMailBox does **not** share, sell, or transmit your data to:

- Advertisers  
- Analytics providers  
- Third‑party services  
- The developer of FreeMailBox  

Your data stays entirely on your device.

---

## **5. How to Delete Your Data**

To delete all FreeMailBox data:

1. Delete the FreeMailBox application  
2. Delete the FreeMailBox configuration directory  
   - Windows: `C:\Users\<User>\FreeMailBox\`  
   - macOS/Linux: `~/FreeMailBox/`

Deleting this folder removes:

- OAuth tokens  
- Cached email  
- All local data

You may also revoke FreeMailBox’s access to Gmail at:

`https://myaccount.google.com/permissions` [(myaccount.google.com in Bing)](https://www.bing.com/search?q="https%3A%2F%2Fmyaccount.google.com%2Fpermissions")
